So how does it work.

Pulse runs on a Rust core, pulling from over one hundred and fifty live sources across twenty two categories. Health, politics, disasters, markets, science, cybersecurity. Every source is normalized into one clean structured block: the fact, the source it came from, and the exact moment it was pulled. A background engine keeps every feed warm on a heartbeat, so the first agent to ask gets the same six millisecond response as the thousandth.

No confidence scores you have to trust. No black box. Just the source, and the timestamp. The same way Chainlink brought verifiable truth to smart contracts, Pulse brings it to agents.

And it is live right now. Not a mockup. A public URL you can open, a working API, and the full source on GitHub. A judge can reproduce every number in this video from a clean browser in thirty seconds.
